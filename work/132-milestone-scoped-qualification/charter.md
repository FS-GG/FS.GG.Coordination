# Milestone-scoped and adaptive qualification Charter

## Identity
- Work id: `132-milestone-scoped-qualification`
- Lifecycle stage: charter
- Status: chartered

## Principles
- Scoped qualification is the ordinary child-unit default; evidence reuse is explicit provenance, never relabelled execution.
- Formal semantic drift, milestone closure, and production-authority boundaries force cold comprehensive qualification.
- Gate cadence is empirical and reversible: observed cost, unique defect yield, detection delay, closure equivalence, and blast radius drive reviewed recommendations.
- Missing, sparse, stale, malformed, or contradictory telemetry never authorizes a weaker cadence.
- One typed qualification plan remains the source for workflow projection, semantic subjects, closure mode, and scheduled review policy.

## Scope Boundaries
- In scope: canonical formal subjects, independently reusable formal artifacts, child versus closure execution modes, exact-head terminal evidence, a generic milestone frontier contract, and approximately daily gate-economics recommendations.
- In scope: deterministic positive and inversion controls for reuse, forced closure, stale/forged evidence, sparse telemetry, high/low yield, and protected-boundary refusal.
- Out of scope: automatic policy weakening, removing comprehensive closure, changing the Quint corpus itself, or optimizing a gate merely because it is slow.
- Out of scope: GS2-04-specific workflow branches; it is the first configured consumer of generic contracts.
- Keep SDD lifecycle ownership separate from optional Governance enforcement.

## Policy Pointers
- ADR-0080 defines scoped child qualification and comprehensive parent closure.
- ADR-0081 defines approximately daily adaptive qualification-cadence review.
- SDD policy comes from `.fsgg/sdd.yml` and `.fsgg/agents.yml`.
- Governance files are optional compatibility pointers and are not evaluated by this command.
